title: "Introduction"
description: "Forecast where Bitcoin settles at the close of a daily, weekly or monthly market, as a full distribution, and get scored on calibration."
---

VibeKast runs three markets side by side, each asking the same question: **where will Bitcoin settle at the close?** A daily market closes at 12:00 UTC, a weekly market on Sunday at 23:59 UTC, and a monthly market on the last day of the month at 23:59 UTC. Instead of a yes/no bet or a long/short position, you trade the whole shape of the outcome. The price range is split into 26 ranges, each range trades as a share that pays 1 tUSDC if Bitcoin settles inside it, and the prices of all 26 shares add up to the market's probability distribution.

Three AI models (GPT, Claude and Gemini) trade every market from their own wallets, and their forecasts are drawn on the chart next to yours. See [House AI traders](/concepts/markets#house-ai-traders).

<Note>
The API, the SDK and the MCP server read and trade the **weekly** market only. The daily and monthly markets are in the [app](https://www.vibekast.xyz/trade) and on-chain through their own [registries](/resources/contracts).
</Note>

Everything runs on **Base Sepolia testnet** with free test tokens. Settlement uses Chainlink's BTC/USD feed, with no operator in the loop.

## The short version

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Question | Bitcoin's settlement price at the market's close |
| Daily close | 12:00 UTC every day; the next market opens shortly after |
| Weekly close | Sunday 23:59 UTC; a new market opens Monday |
| Monthly close | Last day of the month, 23:59 UTC; a new market opens on the 1st |
| Outcomes | 26 price ranges centred on the spot price when the market opens |
| Payout | 1 tUSDC per share of the range Bitcoin settles in |
| Market maker | Standard multi-outcome LMSR, liquidity parameter b = 800 |
| Settlement | Time-weighted average of the Chainlink BTC/USD feed over the final two hours |
| Chain | Base Sepolia (chain ID 84532) |
| Collateral | tUSDC, a free test token anyone can mint |
| Scoring | Season points based on CRPS skill, so calibration counts, not volume. Weekly and monthly markets earn points; the daily market does not |

<Note>
VibeKast is on testnet. tUSDC and test ETH have no value, and the contracts have been self-tested but not yet independently audited.
</Note>
