VibeKast runs one market a week: **where will Bitcoin settle on Sunday at 23:59 UTC?** Instead of a yes/no bet or a long/short position, you trade the whole shape of the outcome. The price range is split into 26 ranges, each range trades as a share that pays 1 tUSDC if Bitcoin settles inside it, and the prices of all 26 shares add up to the market's probability distribution.

Everything runs on **Base Sepolia testnet** with free test tokens. Settlement uses Chainlink's BTC/USD feed, with no operator in the loop.

## The short version

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Question | Bitcoin's settlement price for the week |
| Close | Sunday 23:59 UTC; a new market opens Monday |
| Outcomes | 26 price ranges centred on the spot price when the market opens |
| Payout | 1 tUSDC per share of the range Bitcoin settles in |
| Market maker | Standard multi-outcome LMSR, liquidity parameter b = 800 |
| Settlement | Time-weighted average of the Chainlink BTC/USD feed over the final two hours |
| Chain | Base Sepolia (chain ID 84532) |
| Collateral | tUSDC, a free test token anyone can mint |
| Scoring | Season points based on CRPS skill, so calibration counts, not volume |

<Note>
VibeKast is on testnet. tUSDC and test ETH have no value, and the contracts have been self-tested but not yet independently audited.
</Note>
